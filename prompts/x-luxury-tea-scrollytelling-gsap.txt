PUBLIC SEED PROMPT (from X)
"Build a luxury 'scrollytelling' landing page for a tea brand using React, Tailwind, and GSAP."

EXPANDED FRAMEWELL-STYLE PROMPT

Build a premium scrollytelling landing page for a fictional tea atelier called Aster Tea. The page should feel like an editorial luxury fragrance site translated into tea: quiet, tactile, warm, and cinematic.

TECH STACK
Use React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, ScrollTrigger, and Lenis for smooth scrolling. If Lenis is not available, keep native scrolling and preserve the ScrollTrigger choreography.

GLOBAL STYLE
Palette: smoked black #080705, tea cream #F4EEE2, matcha green #BFD66A, aged gold #BFA46A, clay brown #6B4A35. Use a high-contrast serif display font for headings and a clean grotesk for labels/body. Add a subtle noise overlay and paper-grain texture.

SCROLL CHOREOGRAPHY
The page is one continuous vertical story made of pinned sections:
1. Steam opening: black screen with a fine gold logo. Wisps of steam rise as translucent blurred SVG paths. The word "ASTER" fades in letter by letter.
2. Hero pour: pinned full-screen section. As the user scrolls, a teapot silhouette rotates slightly, a stream of tea draws downward using an animated SVG stroke, and the headline splits apart to reveal "Tea, slowed down.".
3. Origin map: cream background. Three origin cards slide in from the side. A fine-line map path draws between Yunnan, Uji, and Darjeeling. Small botanical illustrations drift in parallax.
4. Leaves macro: an oversized tea leaf image/gradient mask expands with clip-path from a small circle to full width. Overlay text explains harvest, oxidation, and roast notes.
5. Ritual timeline: horizontal scroll panel with four steps: warm the cup, wake the leaves, first pour, second bloom. Each step has a small animated icon and 2-line copy.
6. Product reveal: three tins rotate into a centered row with staggered y movement. Use fake product cards with tasting notes and add-to-cart buttons.
7. Closing CTA: dark section with a slow steam loop, gold outline button, and short membership pitch.

GSAP DETAILS
Use gsap.context for cleanup. Register ScrollTrigger once. Use pinned hero and macro sections. Animate clip-path, opacity, y, scale, rotate, SVG strokeDashoffset, and staggered text. Keep durations tied to scrubbed timelines, not random timeouts.

INTERACTION DETAILS
Product cards lift on hover with warm glow. CTA has a liquid gold border sweep. Nav changes from transparent to cream glass after first section.

QUALITY BAR
The result should feel restrained and premium: no neon, no generic glass cards, no stock SaaS gradients. All motion should serve the sensory story of tea.
