# Framewell brand notes

## Positioning

Framewell is a clean motion-prompt library: a place to browse interface ideas by what they look like before opening the prompt text.

## Name

Framewell combines two ideas:

- frame: interface states, animation frames, visual references
- well: a deep, reusable source of prompts and patterns

## Logo

The mark combines a UI frame/grid with a cursor-like selection wedge. It should feel like a small tool for collecting, judging, and copying web-motion references.

Assets:

- `assets/ui/framewell-mark.svg`
- `assets/ui/framewell-logo.svg`
- `assets/ui/favicon.svg`

## Visual system

- warm paper background
- charcoal text
- thin dividers
- large preview cards
- quiet hover motion
- no empty placeholder thumbnails
- concept sketches only when no source media exists

## Copy rules

Use plain, specific language.

Good:

- Browse by what it looks like.
- Every card has a preview or a clear concept sketch.
- Public and open material, kept with provenance.

Avoid:

- generic AI hype
- vague design words without examples
- claiming concept sketches are real screenshots
